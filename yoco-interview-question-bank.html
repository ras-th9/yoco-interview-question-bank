Interview question bank

A browsable companion to the HiBob scorecards. Pick a stage — and optionally a role or competency — to get suggested questions. Anchors are the must-ask backbone; everything else is optional colour to mix in.

▶ How to use this

Each interview stage in HiBob has a scorecard that defines what you're rating. This bank holds the questions that draw those signals out — kept separate so wording can evolve without touching the scorecards.

  1. Choose the interview stage you're running (e.g. Hiring manager).
  2. Optionally narrow by role/function (e.g. Engineering) and competency (e.g. Problem-solving) for the candidate in front of you.
  3. Cover the ★ Anchor questions first — they map directly to the scorecard. Then add one or two Optional questions to go deeper where it matters.
  4. Hit the copy icon on any card to drop the question into your notes.

Reminder: you score the candidate on the HiBob scorecard, not here. This is just where the questions live.
